# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Pip AI Room

## Objective
Short yc-parody launch video for Pip AI Room (Chrome extension: Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude research together).

## Output
- Composition: `composition/` (single-file `index.html`, GSAP local, no network)
- Render: `brag.mp4`, landscape 1920x1080, 30fps, 21.52s, music + SFX

## Source material
- Project root: ~/agent pip. Read: README.md, src/room/room.html, src/room/room.css, src/core/roles.js, src/core/conductor.js (phases), src/pip/engine.js
- Verbatim copy used: "Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude, researching together."; "Connect all three"; "Start the mission"; "Mission"; roles Scout / Analyst / Reviewer; phases Scope, Collect, Hypothesise, Challenge, Conclude; the mission placeholder question; "No server, no API keys" (README); the room page's hint lines and "Normal · 18 turns".
- Reused real UI: room-page seat cards, mission form, phase tracker, feed (CSS ported from room.css); Pip's real sprite engine (src/pip/engine.js) drives the mascot.

## Creative direction
yc-parody. Deadpan: "You have three AI chatbots. They have never spoken." Hard cuts on the beat (3.52, 7.02, 13.01, 17.52, 20.02). One claim per scene.

## Visual identity
Paper #fffaf3, ink #2e2d2a, pink #e6a0ac / #c9697a; seat colours Gemini #3d68d8, ChatGPT #1e7a50, Claude #a9501f (darkened from the product's for WCAG AA). Serif headings, sans body, mono kickers.

## Audio
happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM) at low level with fade in/out; 51 sparse SFX (key ticks, clicks, soft impacts, one bell) matched to motion; bass-reactive pink glow behind Pip (subtle). Beat locks: 5.03/5.53/6.03 (pills), 8.02/8.52/9.02 (seats), 9.52 (click), 13.51-16.52 (messages), 17.52 and 20.02 (strong cues).
